Hey kiddo,

So I'm a dad, and I like science-fiction. Does it need to get more complicated?

My favourite subgenres are typically positivist and optimistic, "fuck yeah
humans!" rather than the eternally grimdark dystopias that seem to make up a
large portion of this scene&mdash;although I do have a soft spot for 40K and
all things cyberpunk.

I write software for a living, but would rather spend my time on various
hobbies: painting miniatures, building retro-like computers, robots, and
mechanical keyboards, and playing video and boardgames both. I live on Earth
with my partner and our teenage son and my fondest wish/hope is that I'm
somehow granted functional immortality and get to see what our little
species does with itself in the centuries to come.

In the meantime I'm falling through spacetime at the speed of causality like all
the rest.
